Rides About

What is
Gravel Social?

A monthly gravel ride that's open to anyone who wants to come. No invitation required — just show up.

How it works

Every month, a route gets posted here with all the details you need: where to meet, when we start, how long it is, and what kind of terrain to expect. If it sounds good to you, come along. If not, there'll be another one next month.

Rides are social pace, meaning we ride together rather than racing. We regroup at climbs, nobody gets dropped, and we generally try to make it a good morning out rather than a training session.

Who's it for?

Anyone who rides a gravel bike, CX bike, mountain bike, or anything with tyres wide enough for rough roads. You don't need to be fast. You don't need to know anyone. If you can handle the distance and terrain listed for that month's ride, you're welcome.

Each ride listing includes a difficulty note to help you decide if it's right for you at the moment.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Just show up at the meeting point before the start time. We roll on time.

The vibe

Friendly. Low-key. A bit muddy, usually. Coffee afterwards when there's somewhere to get it.

The basics

  • Once a month, usually the second Saturday
  • Routes vary in length and terrain
  • All paces welcome
  • No RSVP, no fee, no fuss
  • Just check the route details and show up

Find us

We don't have a group chat or mailing list on purpose — this site is how you find out about rides. Check back each month.
