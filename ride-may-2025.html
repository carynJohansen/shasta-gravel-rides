Past ride Saturday, 10 May 2025

Hawk Hill to
Fernwood Valley

Distance 62 km
Climbing 900 m
Start time 7:30 am
Difficulty Challenging
Riders 11

How it went

Our biggest ride of the spring didn't disappoint. Eleven riders gathered at Old Station Road on a perfect cool morning, and we made steady work of the long climb through the old-growth section above the valley. The views at the top were worth every metre of elevation.

The fast gravel descent down to Fernwood was the highlight — loose in places but well-signed, and everyone made it down grinning. Coffee and cake at the valley café rounded off a great morning. We were back at the cars by 1pm.
