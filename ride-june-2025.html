Upcoming Saturday, 14 June 2025

Ridgeline Loop
via Clearwater Creek

Distance 48 km
Climbing 650 m
Start time 8:00 am
Difficulty Moderate

About this ride

A classic mix of packed forest roads and open meadow singletrack. We start at the Millbrook Trailhead, climb steadily through the pine belt for the first 12 km, then level out onto the ridge for sweeping views east. One more short push at km 30, then a lovely fast gravel descent back to the cars.

This is a good route for confident beginners — there's nothing technically tricky, and the pace will be social throughout. We'll regroup at the top of the main climb so nobody gets left behind.

Meeting point

📍
Millbrook Trailhead parking lot

End of Mill Creek Road, off Route 7. There's a large gravel lot — look for the green trail kiosk. Free parking.

⏰

We roll at 8:00. Aim to arrive by 7:45 to sort kit and say hello. No waiting after the start time.

What to expect

  • Social pace — we ride together, not as a race
  • Regroup at the top of every main climb
  • No drop policy — we make sure everyone finishes
  • Bring enough food and water for the full distance
  • There's a small stream crossing at km 18 — expect wet feet if it's been raining
