About this ride

A Shasta Valley classic. Stage the cars at the mouth of Slough road [exact location TBD], and ride on well-maintained gravel with some of the best views of Mount Shasta if it's clear. Return to start on Bear Springs Road.

This is a good route for confident beginners — there's nothing technically tricky, and the pace will be social throughout. Hilly with no major climbs.

Meeting point

📍
Slough Road

Take I-5 Exit 753 for Weed Airport Road, then take Edgewood Road until Slough road is on your left. Pullout on the right shortly after the turn.
